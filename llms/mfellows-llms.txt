# Money Fellows

> Money Fellows is an Egyptian fintech that digitizes the traditional rotating savings and credit association (ROSCA / "gam'eya") as app-based "money circles," letting over 8 million users save collectively and access payouts and credit. Regulated by the Central Bank of Egypt and backed by 500 Global.

Money Fellows is a consumer (B2C) mobile-first financial services product, not a developer API platform. It publishes no public developer portal, product API, or SDKs. The only machine-discoverable surface on the domain is the Umbraco CMS Content Delivery API member-authentication server (OpenID Connect / OAuth 2.0) that backs the marketing website.

## Products
- [Money Circles](https://moneyfellows.com): Group savings-and-lending circles with flexible 6/10/12-month durations; loan amounts up to 1,200,000 EGP per circle.
- [Money Fellows Card](https://moneyfellows.com): Spending and savings card with cashback rewards.
- [Goals](https://moneyfellows.com): Link circles to personal financial objectives.
